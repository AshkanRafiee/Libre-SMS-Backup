Back up your SMS, available MMS, and call history without giving the app internet access. Libre SMS Backup is free and open source, with no account, ads, analytics, or tracking.

• Choose what to back up: messages, call logs, or both.
• Back up automatically: schedule daily, weekly, or monthly runs at a time you choose.
• Control what stays: keep the newest backup sets, keep them all, or keep the newest available backup for each day, week, and month within windows you choose. The app trims older sets after a successful backup.
• Protect new backups with a password: AES-256-GCM encryption is optional and off by default. Keep the original password to restore an encrypted archive.
• Restore the records you need: choose messages, calls, or both. The app checks encrypted archives before writing any records and skips detected duplicates.

Choose a local folder to work entirely offline. Libre SMS Backup has no internet permission and does not upload files. A cloud-backed folder may sync through its own provider.

Android may require Libre SMS Backup to temporarily become your default SMS app for restricted messages or SMS/MMS restore. Incoming MMS is not handled while it holds that role. Scheduled backups pause while restricted messages are selected. RCS support is device-dependent; compatible text restores as SMS, not as RCS.
